I tried the new Apple headphones from Cupertino last Thursday, and I am thoroughly disappointed. The build quality feels cheap, the battery life is terrible, and customer support at the local store was rude and unhelpful. By contrast, my older Sony pair purchased in Tokyo still performs admirably. Sarah Johnson at the Amazon returns desk was kind enough to process my refund within minutes, which was a small but bright spot in an otherwise frustrating week. I cannot recommend this product to anyone in Chicago, Austin, or anywhere else.
